# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 26, 2026

## Introduction

Nahuel Ads Manager is an internal tool created for Nahuel to connect Meta Ads data with Hermes for the purpose of analyzing and managing advertising campaigns related to Nahuel's investment-focused social media presence.

This Privacy Policy explains how data accessed through this tool is collected, used, stored, and protected.

## Data Collection and Use

Nahuel Ads Manager may access Meta Ads data only for internal advertising analysis, reporting, optimization, and authorized campaign management.

The data accessed may include:

- Meta ad account information
- Campaign performance metrics
- Campaigns, ad sets, ads, and creative information
- Budget, spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, CTR, CPC, CPA, ROAS, and related advertising metrics
- Campaign configuration data required to analyze or manage advertising performance

This data is used only by the owner of the connected Meta account for internal advertising operations.

**No data is sold, rented, shared with, or disclosed to third parties.**

## Campaign Management

Any campaign management actions performed through the App are intended to be authorized by the owner of the connected Meta account.

The App may be used to analyze campaigns, generate reports, prepare recommendations, and assist with campaign management workflows. Any sensitive or high-impact actions, such as changing budgets, pausing campaigns, creating new campaigns, or activating ads, should be reviewed and explicitly authorized by the account owner.

## Data Storage

Data retrieved through this App may be stored securely on private infrastructure only as needed to provide reporting, dashboards, campaign analysis, alerts, and operational history.

Stored data may include cached API responses, analytics summaries, campaign reports, and operational logs necessary for the App's functionality.

Access tokens and credentials are handled securely and are not publicly exposed.

## Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect data accessed through this App, including secure API connections, private storage, and controlled access to credentials and tokens.
