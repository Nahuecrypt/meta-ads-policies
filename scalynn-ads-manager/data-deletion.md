# User Data Deletion Instructions

**Last updated:** May 26, 2026

## How to Request Data Deletion

If you want data associated with Scalynn Ads Manager deleted, please contact Scalynn directly:

**Email:** nahuelcieri21@gmail.com

Please include enough information to identify the connected Meta account, advertising account, business account, or client account associated with your request.

## What Happens When You Request Deletion

Upon receiving a deletion request, Scalynn will:

1. **Identify** all data associated with the connected Meta account, advertising account, business account, or client account within the App.
2. **Delete** stored access tokens, cached API responses, analytics data, reports, dashboards, and operational records associated with that account when deletion is required and appropriate.
3. **Revoke or disconnect** access where applicable.
4. **Confirm** the deletion by email within 30 days of receiving the request.

## Client Account Data

For client advertising accounts, deletion requests may be submitted by the client, the account owner, or an authorized representative.

Scalynn will delete or disconnect data related to a client account upon request, unless retention is required for legal, financial, security, or legitimate business recordkeeping obligations.

## Automatic Data Handling

- Access tokens are removed or revoked when the App is disconnected from a Meta account.
- Cached data is purged when it is no longer needed for active reporting or operations.
- Data that is no longer necessary for analysis, reporting, alerts, or campaign management is deleted or anonymized when appropriate.
