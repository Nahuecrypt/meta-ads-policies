# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 26, 2026

## Introduction

Scalynn Ads Manager is an internal business tool used by Scalynn to connect Meta Ads data with Hermes for the purpose of analyzing, reporting, and managing advertising campaigns for Scalynn and authorized client ad accounts.

This Privacy Policy explains how data accessed through this tool is collected, used, stored, and protected.

## Data Collection and Use

Scalynn Ads Manager may access Meta Ads data only for authorized advertising analysis, reporting, optimization, and campaign management.

The data accessed may include:

- Meta ad account information
- Campaign performance metrics
- Campaigns, ad sets, ads, and creative information
- Budget, spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, CTR, CPC, CPA, ROAS, and related advertising metrics
- Campaign configuration data required to analyze or manage advertising performance
- Client advertising account data only when access has been explicitly granted by the client or account owner

This data is used exclusively for internal business operations, client reporting, campaign analysis, optimization, alerts, and authorized campaign management.

**No data is sold, rented, or shared with unauthorized third parties.**

## Client Account Access

Scalynn Ads Manager may be connected to client advertising accounts only when Scalynn has been granted permission by the client or authorized account owner.

Any campaign management actions performed through the App are intended to be authorized by Scalynn and/or the relevant account owner.

Sensitive or high-impact actions, such as changing budgets, pausing campaigns, creating new campaigns, or activating ads, should be reviewed and explicitly authorized according to Scalynn's operating procedures.

## Data Storage

Data retrieved through this App may be stored securely on private infrastructure only as needed to provide reporting, dashboards, campaign analysis, alerts, and operational history.

Stored data may include cached API responses, analytics summaries, campaign reports, dashboards, and operational logs necessary for the App's functionality.

Access tokens and credentials are handled securely and are not publicly exposed.

## Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect data accessed through this App, including secure API connections, private storage, controlled access to credentials and tokens, and restricted operational access.
